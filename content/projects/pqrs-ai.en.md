# AI-assisted PQRS

## Summary

I participated in designing an AI-assisted PQRS management proposal to make requests easier to review, classify, and assign.

The experience was designed for AI to support staff by prioritizing requests, generating summaries, and recommending classifications and assignments.

The goal was to integrate these capabilities into a clear interface where each suggestion could be reviewed and modified before being applied, keeping people in control of decisions.

## Role and team

My contribution focused on the UX/UI design of interfaces for AI-assisted request management.

I worked on organizing and presenting recommendations, interactions for modifying individual fields, actions for applying suggestions, and the design of the dynamic email composition module.

The work included defining interface behaviors and developing visual proposals in Figma.

## Context and challenge

Managing a high volume of PQRS requests presents an operational challenge, particularly when reading requests, identifying priorities, classifying cases, and assigning responsibility.

Introducing artificial intelligence offered an opportunity to support these tasks, but also raised a user experience challenge: **how can automated recommendations be presented so they are understandable, easy to review, and editable?**

The challenge was to design an experience that used AI capabilities without turning recommendations into final decisions, allowing staff to retain control of the process.

## Design process

### 1. Organizing the experience

The process began by structuring the main management tasks: viewing requests, identifying priorities, understanding their content, and reviewing classification and assignment recommendations.

Based on these needs, I organized the interface proposal to present relevant information and make available actions easy to access.

### 2. Designing AI-assisted recommendations

Dedicated areas were designed to display AI-generated summaries and suggestions, helping staff quickly understand a request's content and review the associated recommendations.

The proposal presented fields such as request type, subtype, department, and assigned staff member individually, allowing each suggestion to be evaluated separately.

### 3. Defining interactions

The interactions were designed to maintain control over recommendations.

Each field included an individual change option, while the **"Apply all suggested changes"** action supported a scenario in which the staff member agreed with the full set of recommendations.

These decisions aimed to provide flexibility to review and modify information without relying exclusively on automatic classification.

### 4. Designing dynamic emails

I also developed the interface proposal for a dynamic email composition module, incorporating smart tokens to build messages using information available in the system.

The design aimed to make content easier to compose and review before sending.

## Key design decisions

- **AI as a support tool:** present recommendations that support staff decisions without replacing them.
- **Request prioritization:** organize information to make cases requiring attention easier to identify.
- **Content summaries:** provide a synthesis to support reading while keeping the original request available.
- **Field-by-field review:** separate request type, subtype, department, and assigned staff member to support individual evaluation.
- **Control over recommendations:** allow each field to be modified or all suggestions to be applied through an explicit action.
- **Dynamic emails:** incorporate smart tokens to make message composition easier.
- **Clarity and transparency:** design an interface that distinguishes AI recommendations from decisions made by staff.

## Solution

A UX/UI proposal was developed for an AI-assisted PQRS management experience organized around three main components.

### Prioritizing and understanding requests

An experience that presents requests by priority and incorporates AI-generated summaries to support reading and understanding each case.

### Assisted classification and assignment

An interface that displays recommendations for request type, subtype, department, and assigned staff member.

Staff can review suggestions, modify individual fields, or apply the full set of proposed changes.

### Dynamic email composition

A module designed to make communications easier to prepare through smart tokens and dynamic content.

The proposal integrates these features into an experience where artificial intelligence provides support and people retain the ability to review and decide.

## Outcomes and learnings

The project produced a design proposal for integrating artificial intelligence recommendations into a request management process.

One of the main contributions was structuring an experience where prioritization, summaries, and classification suggestions connect to clear review and editing actions.

The process reinforced the importance of designing interactions that help people understand an AI system's recommendations, beyond simply presenting its results.

It was also an opportunity to explore how to integrate artificial intelligence capabilities into institutional tools while keeping human control central to the experience.

## Next steps

As a next step, it would be valuable to conduct usability testing with staff responsible for managing PQRS to evaluate their understanding of recommendations, the clarity of actions, and the ease of modifying information.

It would also be important to validate confirmation states, whether changes are saved, and scenarios in which an AI recommendation is unsuitable.

Subsequently, metrics such as handling time, recommendation accuracy, and correction frequency could be evaluated to guide further iterations.

## Closing

This project allowed me to explore the design of AI-assisted experiences in an institutional context, where clear information and control over decisions are essential.

The main learning was that the value of integrating AI lies in both generating recommendations and designing experiences that allow people to interpret, review, and use them thoughtfully.
