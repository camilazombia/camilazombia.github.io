# Digital campaigns

## Summary

I contributed to the visual development of digital campaigns for Flexi, creating key visual proposals and adaptations for email marketing, Meta ads, and animated assets.

The goal was to create engaging, consistent visual communication, preserving each campaign's identity while adapting it to the characteristics of different channels.

## Role and team

My contribution focused on visual conceptualization, exploring graphic proposals, and designing digital assets.

As a freelance designer, I adapted the main compositions to different formats, paying attention to visual hierarchy, product presentation, and graphic consistency across assets.

## Context and challenge

Commercial seasons called for communicating products and promotions through assets that could capture attention across different platforms.

The main challenge was to maintain a recognizable visual identity when translating the same campaign into formats with different proportions, spaces, and reading patterns, such as emails, posts, ads, and animations.

## Design process

The process began by exploring different key visual proposals, looking for alternative compositions, graphic treatments, and ways to present products for each season.

Based on these visual proposals, I developed adaptations for the selected channels, reorganizing elements and adjusting compositions to suit each format.

Animated assets were also developed to introduce movement into the communication and expand the ways the campaigns could be presented.

## Key design decisions

- **Creative exploration:** develop different visual proposals for each campaign.
- **Graphic consistency:** maintain a recognizable identity across assets.
- **Multiple format adaptation:** adjust compositions, proportions, and hierarchies for email and Meta ads.
- **Product presentation:** highlight commercial elements while preserving visual clarity.
- **Animated content:** incorporate movement as a complementary communication resource.

## Solution

A set of visual proposals and digital assets was developed for seasonal campaigns, including:

- Main campaign key visuals.
- Email marketing assets.
- Digital ads for Meta.
- Adaptations to different sizes and proportions.
- Animated assets for digital communication.

Each format was developed with its specific characteristics in mind, maintaining a visual connection to the campaign's main concept.

## Outcomes and learnings

The project produced different creative proposals and digital adaptations, strengthening the visual consistency of the campaigns across multiple channels.

One of the main learnings was the importance of designing a flexible graphic concept that can adapt to different formats without losing its identity or the clarity of its message.

The experience also strengthened my skills in composition, visual hierarchy, and adapting commercial content for digital media.
