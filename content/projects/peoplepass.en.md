## Key design decisions

- Distinguish social media and POP materials to consider each medium’s context.
- Maintain identity consistency across digital and physical assets.
- Adapt compositions to their formats as part of the graphic design work.
- Keep the narrative focused on documented outputs rather than presenting a product transformation.
