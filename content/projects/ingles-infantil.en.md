# English for children

## Summary

I designed and developed the foundations of an English learning platform for children, organized into 12 worlds with activities, musical stories, songs, and interactive games.

The proposal grew out of benchmarking popular educational apps, complemented by guidance from an expert in English learning methodologies. This process helped define the content structure, interactions, and visual experience of the platform.

## Role and team

My contribution included researching references, developing the opening story, exploring illustration styles, designing interfaces and navigation, and developing the frontend.

For implementation, I used Visual Studio Code, with Claude and Codex as supporting tools. AI also supported audiovisual production. The project was then handed over to a backend developer to continue its integration.

I also took part in validation sessions with children to observe how well they understood the activities and how easily they interacted with the platform.

## Context and challenge

The main challenge was to design an experience that brought different educational resources together in an engaging, intuitive, and progressive journey.

Children needed to be able to identify their activities, understand how to move forward, and stay motivated through visual elements and rewards, taking into account differences in age and familiarity with digital devices.

## Design process

The process began with benchmarking educational platforms, analyzing their interactions, progression systems, content, and visual resources. Guidance from an English teaching expert complemented this research and helped shape the organization of the topics.

Based on these references, I defined the navigation structure, explored illustration styles, and designed the interfaces, entry points, and world experiences.

I then developed the frontend with support from AI tools and took part in validation sessions with children. These sessions showed that understanding and ease of use varied according to age and previous experience with mobile apps.

## Key design decisions

- **World-based progression:** 12 worlds with 6 activities each and a minimum of 16 stars required to complete each world.
- **Varied content:** 16 flashcard topics, 17 musical stories, 12 karaoke songs, and 9 Memory Match activities.
- **Rewards:** coins that children can collect to personalize their character and support their progress.
- **Visual navigation:** recognizable entry points, illustrations, and a simple structure to make exploration easier.
- **Child-focused experience:** interactions designed for different ages and levels of digital familiarity.

## Solution

The platform brings learning and entertainment together in an experience organized into worlds and levels. Each activity is part of a progressive journey where children can collect stars, earn coins, and personalize their character.

The multimedia resources complement the main activities and offer different ways to explore and practice the language.

## Outcomes and learnings

The project brought together a visual and functional proposal with 12 worlds, 72 activities, and complementary multimedia resources, alongside the initial frontend development.

Validation sessions with children revealed differences in ease of interaction depending on age and experience with digital devices, reinforcing the importance of designing flexible, understandable experiences.

The process also made it possible to integrate AI tools into audiovisual production and development while keeping experience and design decisions central to the project.

## Next steps

In a future phase, usability testing could be expanded to different age groups, with further exploration of accessibility, understanding of instructions, and the effectiveness of the educational activities.

These evaluations would help improve the experience based on users' real needs.
