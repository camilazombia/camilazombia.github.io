# Niibu: three AI applications

## Summary

An ecosystem spanning Niibu PRO: Get Booked, Niibu: Personal AI Concierge, and Niibu: Creator Studio. My contribution combined a complete design system with flows organized for development in a fast-moving project.

## Role and team

I developed tokens, components, and templates and organized happy paths, alternative paths, error flows, and edge cases. I also worked on AI feature design. The full team composition is not documented.

## Context and problem

The applications serve different needs: receiving professional bookings, organizing personal activities, and managing creator work. The challenge was to establish consistency without erasing each application’s task structure.

## Process

Development moved quickly, without iterative processes. I researched reference applications and expected flows to inform decisions, structured the design system, and documented journeys for development. Reference research is not presented as user research or usability validation.

## Key design decisions

- Build shared tokens, components, and templates to support consistency across products.
- Document alternative paths, errors, and edge cases alongside the happy path to clarify expected behavior.
- Present each application separately to keep its primary tasks visible.
- Design AI features within defined flows without claiming undocumented accuracy or effectiveness testing.

## Solution

### Niibu PRO: Get Booked

A tool for professionals receiving bookings and managing availability. Its official listing describes schedules, services, and job management.

### Niibu: Personal AI Concierge

An assistant for daily planning, discovering self-care and wellness options, and tracking menstrual cycles, medication, and calories, as described in its official listing.

### Niibu: Creator Studio

The wallet traces earnings to their source. The listing describes verified follower rewards and fraud checks, follower versus non-follower analytics, escrow-backed campaigns, events and collaborations, subscriptions, tagged products, threads, and a portfolio-style profile. These are product capabilities, not financial outcomes of the design.

## Outcomes and learnings

The described deliverables are the design system and organized application flows. No measured improvement in usage, earnings, or development speed is claimed. The takeaway is that documenting states and exceptions makes decisions explicit even when the schedule does not allow iteration.
