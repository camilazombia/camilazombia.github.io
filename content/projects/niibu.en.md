# Niibu: three AI applications

## Summary

I participated in designing three applications in the Niibu ecosystem, each addressing different needs and user profiles.

The project covered experiences for managing professional bookings, organizing personal activities with artificial intelligence support, and managing tools for content creators.

My work included building a design system with tokens, reusable components, and templates, as well as defining main flows, alternative paths, error states, and edge cases.

The main goal was to develop consistent, functional experiences while respecting the specific characteristics of each product and its development needs.

## Role and team

My contribution focused on the UX/UI design of the three applications and the creation of their design system.

I researched reference applications, organized features, designed interfaces, and defined user journeys.

I also developed tokens, reusable components, and templates, and documented different states and interactions to make the designs easier to interpret during development.

The project took place in an agile working environment with tight deadlines, so the process focused on structuring the experiences and preparing design deliverables.

## Context and challenge

Niibu brings together three digital products with distinct goals:

- **Niibu PRO: Get Booked:** for professionals who need to manage services, bookings, and availability.
- **Niibu: Personal AI Concierge:** focused on personal organization and support for everyday activities through artificial intelligence.
- **Niibu: Creator Studio:** for content creators managing their activity, audience, and monetization opportunities.

The main challenge was to design experiences tailored to each product while maintaining a consistent identity and interaction logic throughout the ecosystem.

This was compounded by the need to work within tight deadlines, defining the main screens alongside alternative behaviors, error states, and scenarios that could arise during use.

## Design process

### 1. Reference research

The process began by exploring applications and digital products with features similar to those of each application.

This analysis helped identify navigation patterns, content organization approaches, and interaction solutions that served as references for structuring the experiences.

The research focused on product references and understanding how comparable features were handled in other applications.

### 2. Defining experiences and flows

Based on each product's needs, I organized features and defined its main journeys.

The design covered ideal flows, alternative paths, error states, and edge cases, aiming to clearly represent the different situations that could arise.

This work documented expected interface behavior and prepared the journeys for development.

### 3. Building the design system

I developed a shared design system for the three applications, consisting of tokens, reusable components, and interface templates.

The goal was to establish a consistent visual and functional foundation that maintained common patterns while accommodating each product's specific needs.

Organizing these elements also helped structure the different screens and states.

### 4. Designing interfaces and AI features

With journeys and components defined, I worked on the interface design for each application.

I also participated in designing AI-assisted features, integrating them into the relevant flows as part of the product experience.

The work focused on clear interactions, information organization, and consistency across features.

## Key design decisions

- **Shared design system:** build a foundation of tokens, components, and templates to maintain consistency across applications.
- **Distinct experiences:** adapt navigation and features to each product's goals.
- **Reusable components:** establish patterns that can be used across screens and scenarios.
- **Complete flows:** document main journeys, alternative paths, error states, and edge cases.
- **AI integration:** incorporate intelligent features into defined experiences and interactions.
- **Development clarity:** organize designs and their behavior to make features easier to interpret.
- **Flexibility:** maintain a design structure that can respond to each application's particular needs.

## Solution

UX/UI proposals were developed for the three applications, supported by a shared design system and documentation of their main journeys.

### Niibu PRO: Get Booked

An application for professionals who need to organize their services and manage bookings.

The experience includes features for availability, scheduling, and job management, providing a clear structure for managing professional activity.

### Niibu: Personal AI Concierge

An application focused on organizing personal activities through an AI assistant.

The experience brings together daily planning, discovery of self-care and wellness options, and personal tracking of information such as menstrual cycles, medication, and calories.

The design aimed to organize these tools within a consistent, accessible user experience.

### Niibu: Creator Studio

An application for content creators, with tools to manage their digital activity, audience, and monetization opportunities.

The proposal includes features such as a wallet showing income sources, rewards for verified followers, audience analytics, campaigns, events, collaborations, subscriptions, and tagged products.

It also includes publishing tools and a portfolio-style profile, bringing different creator needs together within a single experience.

### Design system

As part of the work across products, a design system was developed with tokens, reusable components, and templates.

This foundation maintained shared visual and interaction principles across the three products while allowing each application to retain its own features and journeys.

## Outcomes and learnings

The project produced design proposals for three applications with different goals, accompanied by a shared design system and flows documented for development.

One of the main contributions was structuring the experiences to account for main journeys, alternative states, errors, and edge cases.

The work reinforced the importance of building flexible design systems that maintain consistency across products while preserving each one's specific characteristics.

It also strengthened my ability to organize complex features, work within tight deadlines, and design experiences that incorporate artificial intelligence in different usage contexts.

## Next steps

As a next step, it would be valuable to conduct usability testing with each application's user groups to evaluate their understanding of the journeys and ease of completing key tasks.

It would also be useful to validate AI-assisted features, especially the clarity of their responses and interactions within each experience.

As the products evolve, the design system could continue to expand, with component reviews and new patterns documented in response to usage and development needs.

## Closing

This project allowed me to approach the design of a digital ecosystem from a comprehensive perspective, connecting user experiences, design systems, and artificial intelligence features.

The main learning was understanding how to maintain consistency across different products while addressing their specific needs, and how clearly defined flows, components, and states can bring clarity to the development process.
