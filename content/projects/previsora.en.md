# Previsora Seguros: social media

## Summary

I designed graphic assets for Previsora Seguros' social media channels, working with the content creator to explore new ways of presenting the brand's content.

The goal was to broaden the visual variety of the posts, incorporating creative proposals and graphic elements to communicate messages clearly and engagingly while remaining consistent with the company's identity.

## Role and team

My contribution focused on graphic design, exploring visual proposals, and creating social media assets.

I collaborated with the content creator, connecting messages and communication goals with graphic proposals adapted to the digital environment.

The project was part of my work at Buentipo Anchor.

## Context and challenge

Social media communication called for exploring new visual resources to expand the variety of content and present the brand's messages in a more dynamic way.

The main challenge was to develop visually distinctive assets while maintaining consistency with Previsora Seguros' identity and making the messages easy to understand.

## Design process

The process was developed in collaboration with the content creator, using the defined content as a starting point to explore different graphic alternatives.

I developed proposals for composition, visual hierarchy, and information presentation, aiming for clear and engaging communication in each asset.

More dynamic and interactive visual resources were also explored to expand the creative possibilities of social media communication.

## Key design decisions

- **Creative collaboration:** connect editorial content with visual proposals that reinforce each message.
- **Graphic variety:** explore different compositions and resources to diversify the posts.
- **Brand consistency:** maintain a recognizable visual identity across assets.
- **Clear communication:** prioritize visual hierarchy and ease of reading.
- **Dynamic content:** explore graphic resources and interactions that add variety to digital communication.

The project produced different graphic proposals for Previsora Seguros' digital communication, exploring new ways of presenting content on social media.
